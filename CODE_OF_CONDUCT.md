# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory language
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention
- Trolling or sustained disruption
- Publishing private information without explicit permission
- Conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior through the repository's private security reporting channel. Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently ban participants whose behavior is inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

This policy applies in all project spaces and when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.
